Session Hoard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

What we store

Your saved sessions (tab titles and URLs) are stored only in your browser's local storage, on your machine, inside your browser profile. We operate no servers that receive your browsing data. The extension contains no analytics, no tracking, and no remote code.

What leaves your machine

Exactly one thing, and only if you choose to use Pro: when you activate a license key (or the extension revalidates one), the extension contacts our license server (your-license-server.example.com). That request contains only the license key and a random device identifier. It never contains your sessions, tab titles, URLs, or anything you browse.

Your data, your call

You can export everything to a readable JSON file, and delete everything, from the options page at any time. Uninstalling the extension removes it and its data entirely.

Changes

If this policy changes, the extension's store listing will be updated with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will be called out in the release notes.
